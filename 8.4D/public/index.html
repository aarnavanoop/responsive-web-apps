About Happy Paws

Welcome to Happy Paws, where our passion for animals drives everything we do. Founded in 2005, our mission has been to provide a safe, welcoming, and knowledgeable environment for pet owners and their beloved companions. We believe that every pet deserves a happy life, and we're here to support you every step of the way, from adoption to lifelong care.

Our Services

Pet Grooming

Our professional groomers provide top-notch services to keep your pet looking and feeling their best, from stylish cuts to relaxing spa treatments.

Obedience Training

Build a stronger bond with your furry friend through our positive reinforcement training classes, suitable for all ages and breeds.

Doggie Daycare

Need a safe and fun place for your dog to stay while you're away? Our daycare offers supervised play and socialization.
