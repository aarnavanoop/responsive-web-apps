Ice Cream Fun Facts

Ancient Origins

The earliest forms of ice cream date back to ancient China, where a frozen mixture of milk and rice was enjoyed. Roman Emperor Nero also had snow flavored with fruits and honey.

Vanilla's Reign

Despite the huge variety of flavours available, classic vanilla remains the most popular and best-selling ice cream flavour around the world due to its versatility.

Brain Freeze!

That sharp headache from eating ice cream too fast is called sphenopalatine ganglioneuralgia. Pressing your tongue to the roof of your mouth can help relieve it.
